GenomX Advisor

Clinical decision support for pharmacogenomics. GenomX Advisor cross-references a patient's genetic profile and allergy history against a curated drug knowledge base to flag unsafe prescriptions before they reach the patient — with a clear, evidence-backed explanation behind every recommendation.

What is GenomX Advisor?

GenomX Advisor analyzes a patient's genetic markers and documented allergy history against a curated pharmacogenomic knowledge base spanning drugs, genes, and allergy classes. For each medication under consideration, it returns a clear compatibility rating — Safe, Monitor, Dose Adjustment, or Contraindicated — along with the specific gene, allergy, or clinical factor driving that assessment.

Patient data is validated and encoded, a knowledge graph links the patient to the relevant genetic and allergy factors, compatibility is scored against each candidate drug, and a ranked recommendation is returned with full supporting evidence. Every result traces back to a specific, inspectable rule rather than an opaque score.

iSeverity scale used throughout

🟢 Safe No genetic, allergy, or clinical contraindication found.
🟡 Monitor Proceed with closer clinical/lab monitoring.
🟠 Dose adjustment Usable, but dose or agent choice needs adjusting.
🔴 Contraindicated Avoid; use a suggested alternative.

    Prototype notice. GenomX Advisor is a decision-support prototype, not a certified medical device. It uses a curated gene/drug/allergy knowledge base and a transparent, rule-based scoring engine rather than a trained clinical model, so every recommendation is traceable to a specific rule. It must not be used for actual prescribing decisions — consult CPIC/PharmGKB guidelines and a qualified clinician or pharmacist for real pharmacogenomic decisions.
