# Requirements — Research paper
1. THE paper SHALL state a precise task, data, split, metrics and baselines for every experiment, and SHALL report CIs.
2. THE paper SHALL separate: (a) rule-engine fidelity, (b) KG association prediction, (c) preclinical-vs-human DILI analysis, (d) system demonstration.
3. THE paper SHALL NOT claim clinical validity, patient-level ADR prediction, or novelty beyond what the literature matrix supports.
4. EVERY citation SHALL be opened and verified by an author; the literature matrix SHALL be complete before Related Work is written.
5. THE paper SHALL include licences/attribution for ClinPGx, SIDER and TG-GATEs, a limitations section and an ethics/safety statement.
6. All numbers in the paper SHALL be regenerated by scripts from versioned data and seeds.
