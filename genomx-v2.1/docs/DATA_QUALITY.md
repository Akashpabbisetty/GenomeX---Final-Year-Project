## G. Claims from the original prototype that were REMOVED or CORRECTED

- **Removed — unsourced confidence percentages** (75–98 per drug). They implied model accuracy; no source supported them.
- **Removed — "CPIC Level A/B" and "PharmGKB 1A" chips.** CPIC levels were not retrievable (CPIC's table is JavaScript-rendered), so no level is asserted (`evidenceLevel: null`).
- **Removed — uses / mechanism / side-effects / contraindications / alternatives text for all 30 drugs.** Unverified; preserved in `data/unverified/legacy_drug_claims.json` and not displayed.
- **Replaced — the 15 "unverified associations" placeholder** is superseded by the real ClinPGx guideline index (all 15 pairs are covered by published guidelines).
- **Removed — 48 drug–drug interaction pairs** (hand-written, unsourced, substring-matched). Quarantined in `data/unverified/legacy_interactions.json`. Interaction screening is reported as *not configured*.
- **Removed — 76 diagnosis-based rules** (hand-written, unsourced, free-text keyword matching). Quarantined in `data/unverified/legacy_diagnosis_rules.json`.
- **Corrected — oxcarbazepine + HLA-B*15:02** was "dose adjustment"; CPIC 2017 advises avoiding it in naive patients (now `avoid`, naive-conditional).
- **Corrected — codeine pediatric rule** was "contraindicated for everyone under 18". FDA: contraindicated under 12 (and after tonsillectomy/adenoidectomy under 18); ages 12–17 carry a warning for risk factors → now age <12 `avoid`, 12–17 `conditional`.
- **Corrected — codeine** now also covers CYP2D6 poor metabolizers (CPIC: avoid) and breastfeeding (FDA: recommends against).
- **Corrected — azathioprine/TPMT** was "Contraindicated"; CPIC advises dose reduction or an alternative depending on context → `modify`. TPMT and NUDT15 are now separate genes with separate results.
- **Corrected — metformin** used a yes/no "kidney impaired" flag scored as −45 points. It now needs a numeric eGFR and follows the FDA thresholds (<30 contraindicated; 30–45 do not start).
- **Corrected — clopidogrel** applied to any CYP2C19 poor-metabolizer flag; CPIC recommendations depend on indication → definite only for ACS/PCI, otherwise `conditional`.
- **Corrected — cephalexin + penicillin allergy** was "dose adjustment"; cross-reactivity depends on the reaction and structures → `conditional` (review), never a blanket avoid.
- **Corrected — SLCO1B1 / DPYD / TPMT checkboxes** collapsed many alleles into one flag. They are now phenotype results (e.g. DPYD intermediate vs poor).
- **Removed — numeric 0–100 "safety score"** and the GNN/ML "residual risk" term. Severity now comes only from rule categories (see ENGINE.md).
- **Removed — inputs that affected nothing:** name, sex, weight, height/BMI, free-text history notes, generic hepatic "impaired" flag. Pregnancy is still collected but is explicitly reported as *not used*.

## H. Known incomplete coverage within verified rules

- CPIC HLA-A*31:01 (carbamazepine) is not modelled.
- Codeine CYP2D6 intermediate metabolizers: no rule encoded (recommendation not captured here).
- Clopidogrel: only ACS/PCI is encoded as a definite rule; other indications are `conditional`. Alternative agents are not named.
- Thiopurines: indication-specific (malignant vs non-malignant) wording and combined TPMT+NUDT15 handling are not encoded; no dose is calculated.
- DPYD: activity-score-specific advice not encoded; evidence is a secondary summary.
- Statins: only simvastatin and atorvastatin (SLCO1B1); dose limits are summarised from a secondary annotation for atorvastatin; ABCG2/CYP2C9 statin guidance not modelled.
- Metformin: eGFR rules only; contrast-media and other labeled contraindications are not encoded.
- Codeine 12–17 risk factors (obesity, sleep apnea, lung disease, recent tonsillectomy) are not collected.
- Genes appearing in guidelines but absent from the patient-input panel (e.g. ABCB1, COMT, OPRM1…) cannot be entered; those guidelines are listed but never matched.
- Guideline recommendations for non-encoded drug–gene pairs are NOT parsed into actions.
- Drug–drug interactions, drug–disease, hepatic, pregnancy and dosing: no verified rules.
