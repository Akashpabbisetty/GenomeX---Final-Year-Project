GenomX Advisor

Evidence-traceable pharmacogenomic and medication-context screening. Every finding comes from a numbered rule with a cited source; missing data is reported as missing, never as normal.

What this prototype does

GenomX Advisor evaluates a candidate medication against structured patient data using a small, versioned set of rules. Each rule has an ID, a source and a documented action category. Nothing is averaged into a score, and a verified contraindication cannot be diluted by other inputs.

Coverage is deliberately limited: only the gene–drug, renal, age and breastfeeding rules listed in the Methodology tab are active. Other medications are reported as not assessed, not as safe.
